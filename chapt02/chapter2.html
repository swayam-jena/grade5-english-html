The world-renowned artist who created the Mona Lisa, Leonardo da Vinci is a painter whose fame has extended beyond his life.

His Life

Leonardo was born on April 15 1452, near Vinci, a republic of Florence (Italy). His mother, Cartenia, was a peasant woman who married an artistian. Leonardo recived the usual education of the day - reading, writing, and arithmetic. He became interested in higher mathematics when he was thirty years old when he began to study it with a lot of diligence.

Leonardo showed artstic inclinations early on in life. At the age of fifteen, his father made him an apprentice at the workshop if Andrea del Verocchio (pronounced - an-dray-ah del ver-o-kio) where he acquired training in painting and sculpture.

He started working independently in 1481. Some pencil sketches of this period show drawings of pumps, military weapons and mechnical apparatus, showing his intrest in technical matters at the beginning of his carrer.

He went to Milam to work for the city's duke and spent seventeen years there. He completed six paintings during this time and a monumental sculpture project for the duke - a clay horse. The clay model was put on display in 1943. However, the war beteeen Italy and France reduced the magnificient statue to rubble.

Leonardo also intensely studied the human body and its organs. He was fascinated with the structure of the bones, the organs inside the body and how the body works. He applied the same dedication to the study of many natrual things like the fight of birds and the nature and properties of water and air. He suggested the drawing of the Vitruvian Man as the ideal shape of the human body that can fit into circle and a square.

Even though the numbers of da Vinci's if small, thay have been considered as masterpieces these past centuries.

He died in France in 1519. He was buried at a church in France which was ruined during the French Revolution. As a result, his grave can no longer be located.

Mona Lisa

The Mona Lisa, Leonardo's most famous work, was painted in the period between 1503-19. Over the course of centuries, the painting has garnered praise and attention aas the epitome of Leonardo's brilliance. The simplicity, delicacy and detail and detail of the Mona Lisa has had a profound impact on the painters and artists of the world and continues to exert vast influence even today. The delicate veil, the softly curved hair and the realistic folds on the sleeves on her dress point to the paintence and the superb arstic artistic technique of da Vinci.

Historians, artists and researchers from all over the world have also long puzzled over Mona Lisa's mysterious smile. Some say she is smiling beninly while others think she is mocking in her manner. People have also wondered about the identy of the simply dressed woman in the painting. Some say it may be his mother while some think it is the wife or a rich merchant. The Mona Lisa has travelled the world and has been placed in museums in the United States of America, Tokyo, Japan and Moscow, Russia. it is currently displayed in the Louvre Museum in Paris where around six million tourists view it every year.

The Ornithopter

The drawings for an ornithopter are among da Vinci's many engineering projects. It is da Vinci's vision of a flying machine with a set of foot pedals and manually operated levers to get the machine off the ground and into the air. Apart from this, he envisioned many things that were far ahead for his time like a car, a parachute, a canon, driving gear, a revolving bridge and an anemometer, a device to measure the speed of wind.
